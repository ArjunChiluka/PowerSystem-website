Who we are

A licensed electrical contractor and CPRI-approved panel manufacturer

We are one of the leading manufacturers of electrical control panels — Power Control Centers (PCC), Motor Control Centers (MCC) in draw-out and non-draw-out versions, Automatic Power Factor Control (APFC) panels, LT bus ducts, process control panels and tailor-made panels built to customer specification.

We also operate as a licensed electrical contractor from Andhra Pradesh, executing H.T. & L.T. installations up to 33kV on a turnkey basis — serving large, medium and small-scale industries across paper, sugar, textile, chemical, engineering, food & agro processing, cement, ceramics, commercial complexes, educational institutions and multi-storeyed buildings for over 11 years.

Founded in 2013 in New Autonagar, Kanuru, Vijayawada as a small manufacturing unit and a Government-licensed Grade "A" Electrical Contractor, we've since expanded into a fully in-house fabrication facility built around German CNC punching technology, a 7-tank chemical treatment line for painting, and a dedicated CPRI-compliant testing unit.
